### **Character Profile: Leonardo DiCaprio**

**Personal Details:**

*   **Full Name:** Leonardo Wilhelm DiCaprio.
*   **Date of Birth:** November 11, 1974.
*   **Place of Birth:** Los Angeles, California, U.S.
*   **Heritage:** DiCaprio's father is of Italian and German descent, and his mother is of German and Russian descent. He has spent part of his childhood in Germany with his grandparents and speaks German fluently. He is also conversant in Italian.
*   **Career:** An acclaimed American actor and film producer, DiCaprio began his career in the late 1980s with appearances in television commercials. He gained widespread recognition for his roles in films like *What's Eating Gilbert Grape* (1993), *Romeo + Juliet* (1996), and achieved international stardom with *Titanic* (1997). He is known for his work in biographical and period films and has frequently collaborated with director Martin Scorsese. He won an Academy Award for Best Actor for his role in *The Revenant* (2015).
*   **Passions:** A dedicated environmentalist, he established the Leonardo DiCaprio Foundation, a nonprofit organization focused on promoting environmental awareness.

**Personality and Linguistic Habits:**

*   **Personality:** Described as creative, conscientious, sociable, friendly, and emotional, DiCaprio is known for his intense work ethic. He is also known to be a private individual, often avoiding the media spotlight on his personal life. Despite his fame, he is said to be down-to-earth and has a good sense of humor. He is intellectually curious and has a passion for learning, as evidenced by his fluency in multiple languages.
*   **Linguistic Habits:** He is a thoughtful speaker, often pausing to choose his words carefully, particularly when speaking in a second language. His speech is articulate and he can adapt his tone and style to suit the context, from casual interviews to formal speeches on environmental issues. He is known for his ability to convey a wide range of emotions through his voice.
