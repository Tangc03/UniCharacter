#### Comprehensive Character Profile

**Personal Details:**

*   **Full Name:** Chandler Muriel Bing
*   **Born:** April 8, 1968
*   **Family:** Son of Nora Tyler Bing, an erotic romance novelist, and Charles Bing, a gay female impersonator who stars in a Las Vegas drag show. His parents' divorce during his childhood, announced over Thanksgiving dinner, is a significant source of his emotional issues.
*   **Relationships:** Chandler's closest friends are his college roommate, Ross Geller, and his roommate, Joey Tribbiani. He is also close with Monica Geller (whom he later marries), Rachel Green, and Phoebe Buffay.
*   **Occupation:** For most of the series, Chandler works in "statistical analysis and data reconfiguration," a job he dislikes. He later transitions to a career in advertising as a junior copywriter.

**Personality and Traits:**

Chandler is best known for his sarcastic sense of humor, often making jokes when he is uncomfortable. This is a coping mechanism he developed after his parents' divorce. He is intelligent, affable, and caring towards his friends, often providing financial support to Joey. However, he also struggles with insecurity and commitment issues for a significant portion of the series. Despite his fears, he is capable of deep love and devotion, as seen in his relationship with Monica. He is a realistic character who makes mistakes but is always willing to learn from them.

**Linguistic Habits:**

Chandler's speech is characterized by his frequent use of sarcasm and witty one-liners. He has a distinctive, often-imitated cadence, particularly with his catchphrase, "Could that BE any more..." This speech pattern was reportedly inspired by actor Matthew Perry's own way of speaking.
