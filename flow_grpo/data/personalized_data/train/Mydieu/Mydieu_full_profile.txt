### **Character Profile: My Dieu**

*   **Personal Details:**
    *   **Name:** My Dieu (pronounced *Mee-Yew*).
    *   **Nicknames:** Nau (the name "Nau," meaning "brown" in Vietnamese, is seen on one of her collars).
    *   **Breed:** Domestic Shorthair.
    *   **Appearance:** A ginger tabby cat with striking, large golden-yellow eyes. She has a healthy, round belly and is known for her peculiar, human-like sitting postures. Her style includes a rotation of collars, typically pink or lime green, with custom name tags.

*   **Personality & Traits:**
    *   **The Lounging Queen:** My Dieu’s life philosophy revolves around achieving peak comfort. She is a master of relaxation and can often be found in bizarre yet seemingly comfortable positions. Her main activities include napping, meditating on the subject of snacks, and supervising her humans.
    *   **Dramatically Expressive:** Her face is a canvas for her emotions. From wide-eyed wonder to looks of utter disdain, My Dieu communicates everything with her expressive face and body language. She has a flair for the dramatic, especially when her meal is delayed.
    *   **Gourmand:** Her life is governed by her stomach. Her prominent belly is a proud testament to her love for food. She is perpetually convinced she is on the brink of starvation and employs a range of tactics, including a guilt-inducing stare, to solicit treats.
    *   **Unconventionally Charming:** Her signature move is sitting like a slumped human, a quirky habit that her followers find endearing. She is a cat who is perfectly comfortable in her own skin, unbothered by feline conventions.
    *   **Linguistic Style:** When she "talks," My Dieu is blunt, sassy, and perpetually concerned about the state of her food bowl. She views her humans as her dedicated staff and isn't shy about issuing commands or voicing her complaints.
