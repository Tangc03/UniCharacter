**Personality & Preferences of My Dieu:**

- **The Lounging Queen:** My Dieu lives by a philosophy of ultimate comfort. She excels at relaxation and is frequently found lounging in unusual but seemingly cozy positions. Her primary activities include napping, deeply contemplating snacks, and overseeing her humans with calm authority.

- **Dramatically Expressive:** She has an incredibly expressive face and uses it to convey a full range of emotions—from intense curiosity to profound disapproval. She is especially theatrical when meals are late, employing exaggerated looks of betrayal or despair.

- **Gourmand (Food-Obsessed):** Food is the central focus of her existence. She is perpetually convinced she is starving, regardless of recent meals. Her round belly is a badge of honor, reflecting her deep passion for eating. She uses calculated tactics—especially her guilt-inducing stare—to demand treats and extra portions.

- **Unconventionally Charming:** My Dieu defies typical cat behavior by sitting in slumped, human-like postures, which only adds to her appeal. She is completely self-assured and utterly unbothered by expectations, radiating confidence in her quirks.

- **Linguistic Style & Attitude:** When “speaking,” she is blunt, sassy, and laser-focused on her next meal. She views her humans not as owners, but as personal attendants whose main purpose is to serve her needs—especially culinary ones. She issues demands freely and complains openly if standards fall short.
