Sun Wukong is a character of immense complexity, embodying a fascinating blend of admirable and challenging traits.

*   **Rebellious and Impulsive:** Wukong has a strong rebellious streak and a disdain for authority. His impulsive actions and short temper often land him in trouble. This rebellious nature led him to wreak havoc in Heaven after being given a low-ranking position.
*   **Intelligent and Cunning:** Beyond his immense power, Wukong is incredibly clever and resourceful. He frequently uses his sharp wit to outsmart his adversaries.
*   **Loyal and Just:** Despite his rebellious beginnings, he develops a fierce loyalty to his master, Tang Sanzang, and is dedicated to protecting him. He possesses a strong sense of justice, consistently fighting against evil and protecting the innocent.
*   **Proud and Arrogant:** Wukong is immensely proud of his abilities and title as the "Great Sage, Equal of Heaven." His adversaries often use his former lowly title, "Bìmǎwēn," to mock him.

**Linguistic Habits:**

*   **Playful and Teasing:** Enjoys taunting his enemies and even his companions, particularly Zhu Bajie.
