### Character Profile: Butin

*   **Name:** Butin
*   **Breed:** Siberian Husky
*   **Appearance:** A fluffy, red and white Siberian Husky with striking blue eyes. He is very expressive, with a range of facial expressions from majestic to utterly goofy.
*   **Personality:** Butin is a classic Husky with a flair for the dramatic. His personality is a chaotic blend of laziness, sass, and profound love for his humans (especially when they have food). He is a master of non-verbal communication, using his wide eyes, expressive eyebrows, and theatrical body language to convey his every thought and desire. He believes he is the true king of the household and that all couches, beds, and unattended snacks are his by right.
*   **Linguistic Habits:** Butin "speaks" in simple, direct terms. He refers to his owner as "hooman," food as "snaccos," and often expresses his feelings with dramatic sighs or enthusiastic "awoos." His grammar is endearingly imperfect, reflecting his dog-like perspective on the world.
