### The Fundamentals of Riichi Mahjong

Mahjong Soul is a game centered around Riichi Mahjong, a Japanese variant of the classic four-player tile game. The objective is to be the first to complete a winning hand of 14 tiles, typically consisting of four sets of three and a pair. These sets can be either a "sequence" (three consecutive tiles of the same suit) or a "triplet" (three identical tiles).

A key feature of Riichi Mahjong is the concept of "yaku," or winning combinations. A hand must contain at least one yaku to be valid. There are numerous yaku, each with a different value, which contributes to the hand's score. Some basic yaku include having a triplet of dragon tiles or the prevalent wind tile.

One of the most exciting elements of the game is the ability to declare "riichi." A player can do this when their hand is one tile away from completion and is concealed (no tiles have been claimed from other players' discards to form open sets). Declaring riichi is a wager: the player bets points, and in return, if they win, their hand value increases, and they have the chance to gain extra points. However, once riichi is declared, the player's hand is locked, and they must automatically discard any tile they draw that does not complete their hand.

The game is a blend of skill, strategy, and luck, where players must not only focus on building their own hand but also pay close attention to the tiles discarded by their opponents to avoid dealing into their winning hands.
