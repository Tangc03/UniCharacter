## The Fundamentals of Riichi Mahjong

Riichi Mahjong, the variant of mahjong played in Mahjong Soul, is a game of skill, strategy, and a touch of luck. The primary objective is to form a winning hand of 14 tiles, typically consisting of four sets (either sequences or triplets) and a pair. What sets Riichi Mahjong apart is the concept of "yaku," or winning combinations, which are necessary to declare a victory.

A standard game is played with 136 tiles, including three suits (Manzu/Characters, Pinzu/Circles, and Souzu/Bamboo) numbered one through nine, as well as Honor tiles (Winds and Dragons). Each player starts with 13 tiles and, on their turn, draws a tile from the wall and discards one.

Key gameplay elements include:

* **Calls:** Players can call "Pon" to form a triplet with a tile discarded by another player, "Chi" to form a sequence with a tile discarded by the player to their left, and "Kan" to form a quad. Making these calls opens a portion of the player's hand.
* **Riichi:** If a player has a concealed hand (no open calls) and is one tile away from a winning hand ("tenpai"), they can declare "Riichi." This is a powerful move that wagers a 1,000-point stick. Upon winning, the player gains the potential for extra points from "ura-dora" (hidden bonus tiles). After declaring Riichi, the player's hand is locked, and they must automatically discard any tile drawn that does not complete their hand.
* **Dora:** These are bonus tiles that add value (han) to a winning hand. The dora indicator is a tile flipped over on the dead wall, and the actual dora is the next tile in sequence. For example, if the indicator is a 3 of Circles, the dora is the 4 of Circles.
* **Yaku:** A winning hand must have at least one yaku. There are numerous yaku, ranging from simple ones like "Riichi" itself and "Tanyao" (a hand with no terminal or honor tiles) to more complex and high-scoring ones like "Chinitsu" (a hand composed entirely of one suit).

The game is typically played over several rounds, with players aiming to accumulate the most points by the end of the match.
