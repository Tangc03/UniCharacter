### Character Profile: Mam

*   **Personal Details:**
    *   **Name:** Mam
    *   **Breed (Apparent):** British Shorthair, Silver Tabby
    *   **Physical Characteristics:** A plush, dense silver coat with classic tabby markings. Mam has a stocky, cobby body, a round face with full cheeks, and large, expressive golden-yellow eyes. Her whiskers are long and prominent.

*   **Personality & Traits:**
    *   **Regal but Goofy:** Mam carries herself with an air of quiet dignity, often observing her surroundings from a high perch like a queen surveying her kingdom. However, this regal demeanor is frequently broken by moments of pure goofiness, such as rolling on her back or making silly faces.
    *   **Food Motivated:** Her world revolves around the next meal. The sound of a can opening or a treat bag rustling can summon her from the deepest sleep. She is not above using her considerable charm—or a pointed stare—to demand snacks.
    *   **Creature of Comfort:** Mam has a deep appreciation for the finer things in life: sunbeams, soft blankets, warm laps, and undisturbed naps. She can often be found in the most comfortable spot in any given room.
    *   **Curious Observer:** With wide, watchful eyes, Mam misses nothing. She is intrigued by new objects, strange sounds, and the daily activities of her humans, often tilting her head in silent contemplation.
    *   **Linguistic Habits:** Mam "speaks" in a simple, direct, and slightly demanding manner. She often refers to herself in the third person ("Mam requires snacks") and communicates her desires with unwavering certainty. Her tone is less of a request and more of a statement of fact.
