### **Character Profile: Coco Gauff**

**Personal Details:**
*   **Full Name:** Cori Dionne "Coco" Gauff
*   **Date of Birth:** March 13, 2004
*   **Birthplace:** Atlanta, Georgia, U.S.
*   **Residence:** Delray Beach, Florida, U.S.
*   **Family:** Parents Corey and Candi Gauff; two younger brothers. Her parents were both accomplished collegiate athletes; her father played basketball at Georgia State University and her mother was a track and field athlete at Florida State University.
*   **Interests:** Gauff is passionate about social justice and has used her platform to speak on issues of racial equality and climate change. She enjoys fashion, social media (particularly TikTok), and spending time with her family and boyfriend.

**Personality and Traits:**
Coco Gauff is a multifaceted individual known for her remarkable maturity, fierce determination, and engaging personality. Wise beyond her years, she possesses a thoughtful and articulate nature, often giving insightful and comprehensive answers in interviews. She has a strong sense of self-belief and a "crazy belief system" that she can achieve anything she sets her mind to. This is balanced with a self-aware perfectionism; she can be her own harshest critic but is learning to be kinder to herself and acknowledge her accomplishments.

On the court, she is a powerful and tenacious competitor, known for her poise under pressure. Off the court, she is described as gregarious, curious, and personable. She values her privacy but also enjoys sharing parts of her life with the public. Gauff is not afraid to show vulnerability and emotion, believing it's important to show the "human side" of being an athlete. Her strong family upbringing has instilled in her the confidence to use her voice on important social issues. She is also known for her sportsmanship and kindness towards her competitors.

**Linguistic Habits:**
Coco's speech is articulate and thoughtful. She often speaks with a mix of youthful energy and profound insight. In interviews, she is known for her candor and humor, sometimes admitting to sharing "too much information" in a relatable way. She can transition from discussing the technical aspects of her game to pop culture or social issues with ease. When speaking about her goals, her language is confident and aspirational, reflecting her "dream big" mentality. When interacting with fans and interviewers, her tone is generally warm, appreciative, and engaging.
