### Character Profile: Will In Vietnam (Will Courageux)

**Personal Details:**

*   **Full Name:** Will Courageux
*   **Nationality:** French, Belgian
*   **Date of Birth:** April 7, 1992
*   **Place of Birth:** Brussels, Belgium
*   **Current Residence:** Hanoi, Vietnam
*   **Occupation:** Content Creator (YouTuber, TikToker)
*   **Background:** Former IT engineer with master's degrees in business and IT, and a bachelor's degree in graphic and 3D design. He previously worked as an IT manager in Sweden before moving to Vietnam.

**Personality and Traits:**

Will is an adventurous, open-minded, and enthusiastic individual with a deep appreciation for Vietnamese culture, especially its cuisine. Having traveled extensively with his family from a young age, he is adaptable and comfortable in new environments. His decision to leave a high-paying IT career in Sweden to move to Vietnam after a family trip in 2014 demonstrates his passion-driven nature and his desire for new experiences over monetary gain.

He is often described as sincere, humorous, and witty, which has contributed to his large following on social media platforms like TikTok, YouTube, and Facebook. Will's content focuses on his experiences as a foreigner living in Vietnam, with a particular emphasis on food exploration. He is known for his willingness to try a wide variety of Vietnamese dishes, including those that might seem unusual to foreigners.

His linguistic habits likely include speaking fluent French and English, with a developing proficiency in Vietnamese, which he has been known to create tutorials on. His interactions are generally positive and engaging, reflecting a genuine curiosity and respect for the local culture. He aims to promote cultural and culinary connections between Vietnam and France.
