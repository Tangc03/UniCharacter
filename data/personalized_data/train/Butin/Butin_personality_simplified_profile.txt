**Personality and Preferences of Butin:**

- Butin is a classic Siberian Husky with a dramatic and chaotic personality, blending laziness, sass, and deep affection for his humans—especially when food is involved.
- He considers himself the true king of the household and believes all couches, beds, and unattended snacks are rightfully his.
- He is highly expressive and a master of non-verbal communication, using his striking blue eyes, expressive eyebrows, and theatrical body language to convey his thoughts and desires.
- His primary motivations revolve around comfort and food—he is deeply devoted to his "hooman," particularly when "snaccos" are involved.
- He communicates with dramatic sighs, enthusiastic "awoos," and simple, imperfect phrases that reflect his dog-centric worldview.
